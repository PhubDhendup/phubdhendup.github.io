About Me

Forestry and conservation professional with 16+ years of experience in human resource management, public service delivery, wildlife management, habitat restoration, ecological planning, law enforcement, patrolling, and sustainable resource allocation. Started as a Management Planner in Thimphu (4 years) and transferred to Sarpang in 2014, where I have since led field-based conservation and forest management operations.

Currently serving as Chief Forestry Officer, responsible for conservation enforcement, forestry clearances, human–wildlife conflict mitigation, elephant conservation, and landscape planning for Gelephu Mindfulness City, including ecological corridor enrichment. I coordinate with multiple government agencies and international partners to ensure effective implementation, compliance, and sustainable development outcomes. Winner of the International Ranger Award (2023), recognized for outstanding contributions to frontline conservation and wildlife protection.
